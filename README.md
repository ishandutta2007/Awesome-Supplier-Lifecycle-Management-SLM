# Awesome-Supplier-Lifecycle-Management-SLM

## Top Supplier Lifecycle Management (SLM) Ecosystem



**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  

*Focused on Supplier Onboarding, Performance Management & Procurement Lifecycle Automation*  

**Last updated: October 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Supplier Lifecycle Management (SLM)**. These tools enable organizations to manage the complete supplier journey—from initial registration and qualification through performance monitoring, compliance tracking, and offboarding—while integrating with procurement, risk, and contract management workflows.



**Examples** include ServiceNow Supplier Lifecycle Management, Coupa, SAP Ariba, Ivalua, Jaggaer, GEP SMART, Apex Analytix, Tealbook, Graphite Connect, and HICX (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: This section is expanded with active projects for self-hosted supplier relationship management, procurement automation, and vendor qualification—ideal for organizations building sovereign supplier lifecycle infrastructure without per-seat licensing lock-in.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms



- **[ServiceNow Supplier Lifecycle Management](https://www.servicenow.com/)**  

  Enterprise SLM application built on the Now Platform, covering supplier onboarding, qualification, performance management, and document compliance; recent Yokohama release adds Now Assist AI summarization, conversational intake for suppliers, flexible KPI tracking with action plans for underperforming suppliers, and many-to-many contact mapping for parent-subsidiary structures .



- **[Coupa](https://www.coupa.com/)**  

  Business spend management platform with supplier information management, onboarding, risk scoring, and performance tracking integrated with procurement and invoicing workflows.



- **[SAP Ariba](https://www.ariba.com/)**  

  Comprehensive supplier lifecycle and procurement network with supplier qualification, onboarding, performance management, and risk monitoring across the Ariba Network.



- **[Ivalua](https://www.ivalua.com/)**  

  Source-to-pay platform with supplier lifecycle management, onboarding portals, diversity tracking, and performance scorecards for enterprise procurement teams.



- **[Jaggaer](https://www.jaggaer.com/)**  

  Spend management suite with supplier management, onboarding, risk assessment, and performance monitoring for direct and indirect procurement.



- **[GEP SMART](https://www.gep.com/)**  

  Unified procurement platform with supplier lifecycle management, onboarding, qualification, and performance tracking integrated with sourcing and contract management.



- **[Apex Analytix](https://www.apexanalytix.com/)**  

  Supplier management and recovery audit platform with supplier onboarding, data validation, and compliance monitoring for AP and procurement teams.



- **[Tealbook](https://www.tealbook.com/)**  

  Supplier data platform with AI-powered supplier discovery, enrichment, and diversity classification to improve supplier lifecycle visibility and onboarding speed.



- **[Graphite Connect](https://www.graphiteconnect.com/)**  

  Supplier engagement and onboarding platform that centralizes supplier information, compliance documents, and communication for procurement teams.



- **[HICX](https://www.hicx.com/)**  

  Supplier experience management platform focused on supplier master data management, onboarding, and self-service portals to reduce supplier friction.



## Open-Source GitHub Projects



- **[mcp-supplier](https://github.com/zavora-ai/mcp-supplier)**  

  Open-source Supplier Relationship Management (SRM) platform with 34 MCP tools covering the full supply base lifecycle: supplier registration and qualification (prospect → active → suspended/disqualified), ISO/IATF/HACCP certification expiry tracking, multi-supplier catalog pricing, RFQ sourcing with quote comparison, purchase orders with approval gates, quality audits with auto-SCAR on failure, performance scorecards (OTD, quality rate, PPM), and supply-risk monitoring; includes full audit trail and governance gates on high-impact writes .



- **[VendorBridge AI](https://github.com/vatsalgajera-tech/VendorBridge-AI)**  

  AI-powered procurement ERP covering vendor management with risk scoring, RFQ creation and multi-vendor assignment, quotation comparison with AI ranking, multi-stage approval workflows, purchase order generation from accepted quotes, invoice management, and activity audit logs; built with React/Node.js/MongoDB .



- **[Shillinq](https://github.com/ConductionNL/shillinq)**  

  Open-source business administration suite for Nextcloud (EUPL-1.2) with procurement management, supplier qualification with configurable questionnaires and document requirements, supplier document management with certificate and compliance tracking, purchase order revision workflows, and contract repository with full-text search .



- **[Dolibarr Supplier Qualification Pro](https://wiki.dolibarr.org/index.php/Supplier_Qualification_Pro)**  

  Commercial module for Dolibarr ERP/CRM that adds weighted supplier evaluation, Approved Supplier List (ASL) management, supplier documents and certificates with expiry monitoring, supplier audits with findings and CAPA follow-up, and role-based permissions for qualification decisions .



- **[Procuman Community Edition](https://github.com/procuman/procuman-community-edition)**  

  Free e-procurement extension for EspoCRM covering supplier management with categorization and relationship tracking, product catalog with supplier associations, and purchase order creation with automated calculations and PDF generation; professional edition adds requisitions, supplier portal, and contract management .



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options



- **Vendor Management System (SevenSquare)** — Laravel/Electron desktop application for managing vendors, contracts with renewal alerts, and purchase orders with status tracking .

- **Open mSupply** — Open-source supply chain software (free for non-commercial use in developing countries) with supplier quotation recording, tenders, purchasing, and inventory management .

- **mcp-procurement** — Complementary MCP server for procurement intelligence including supplier risk scoring, price benchmarking, contract clause checking, and negotiation briefs .



**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Combine **mcp-supplier** for full SRM lifecycle with qualification gates and quality management, **VendorBridge AI** for procurement workflow automation with AI-assisted quote comparison, and **Shillinq** for Nextcloud-integrated supplier document compliance in a self-hosted supplier lifecycle management stack.

## Disclaimer



- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.

- Supplier lifecycle platforms handling vendor and procurement data must comply with applicable regulations (GDPR, anti-corruption laws, industry-specific compliance requirements).

- Self-hosted open-source solutions require proper security hardening, integration planning with ERP/finance systems, and workflow customization for qualification and approval gates.

- Verify license terms for commercial use; mcp-supplier is an open-source MCP server, Shillinq uses EUPL-1.2, and Dolibarr modules may have separate commercial licensing.
